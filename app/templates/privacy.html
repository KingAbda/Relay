Privacy Policy

Version 2026-07-13-draft · External legal review: not yet evidenced

What we collect

We collect your name, approved university email address, optional school profile details, skills and learning interests, consent records, session details, credit-ledger activity, blocks, reports, disputes, and moderation outcomes. Meeting details are visible only to the two participants and authorized moderators. The public browse preview does not expose participant profiles or contact details. Relay does not collect payment information, government IDs, precise device location, advertising identifiers, or profile images during this trial.

How we use it

We use this data only to verify trial eligibility, run the skill exchange, maintain the exact credit ledger, send necessary account and session messages, prevent abuse, respond to safety reports, and evaluate the controlled trial. We do not sell participant data or use it for advertising. Operational email is sent through the configured delivery provider, which processes the destination address and message needed for delivery. The production provider is a launch gate and has not yet been evidenced.

Data retention

Direct account and profile data is kept while your account is active. You can download a JSON copy or close your account from Edit Profile; direct profile identifiers are replaced immediately. Linked ledger, session, consent, safety, dispute, and moderation records remain under an opaque deleted-account ID and are therefore pseudonymized, not guaranteed anonymous. Free-text safety evidence may remain identifying and is access-restricted. These limited records are retained for 24 months after the controlled trial closes, then deleted by the operator. This retention supports ledger integrity, safety follow-up, and auditability. A verified production backup and restore process is still a launch gate; once configured, expired backup copies must rotate out within 30 days.

Cookies

Relay uses an essential signed session cookie containing an opaque user identifier and session version. It is HTTP-only and same-site restricted, and is secure-only in production. We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party cookies. Application assets are served by Relay rather than sending email-derived avatar requests to third-party services.

Security

Passwords and one-time account secrets are stored as hashes. Production startup requires HTTPS-facing configuration, trusted hosts, a persistent database, and shared rate limiting. Login attempts are limited and repeated failures temporarily lock the account. No security program can eliminate all risk; the trial remains blocked until infrastructure and recovery gates are evidenced.

Contact

Questions or requests that cannot be completed in-product should go to {{ contact_email }}. A named, monitored support owner and response schedule must be confirmed before invitations are sent.

Your controls. You can download your data, correct your profile, or pseudonymize and close your account in-product. This draft is not a substitute for the required external legal/privacy review.
