How credits work

Time is the currency.

Every exchange is simple enough to explain in one sentence: teach for 30 minutes, earn a credit, spend it on another 30-minute lesson.

Controlled trial scope

Creative skills, invited participants, no payments.

Relay is preparing a 10–20 person NYU trial limited to vetted creative-skill listings. Full profiles and booking are available only after invitation and email verification. Referrals, proof rewards, ambassadors, recurring bookings, public request claiming, and paid plans are not part of this trial.

Honest answers

The questions students ask first.

1Is Relay free?

Joining is free. Students trade credits for sessions, so no cash changes hands between peers.

2What if I only want to learn?

Verified invited participants receive {{ relay_starter_credits }} starter credits, then earn credits only by completing teaching sessions.

3How do credits work?

Teach one 30-minute session to earn one credit. Spend one credit to book a 30-minute session with another student.

4What can I teach?

The controlled trial accepts vetted creative-skill listings only. High-risk, medical, weapons-related, illegal, or dangerously physical instruction is prohibited.

5How do I know the teacher is good?

Only verified invited participants can open full profiles. Reviews appear only after completed sessions.

6Why not YouTube or AI?

Relay is for live creative feedback: someone reacting to your draft, demonstrating a technique, or practicing with you in real time.

7Who can join right now?

The first cohort is limited to 10–20 manually invited NYU participants who verify their institutional email.

8What happens if someone flakes?

During the controlled trial, cancellation before completion returns the single held credit once.
