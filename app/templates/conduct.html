Code of Conduct

Version 2026-07-13-draft · External legal review: not yet evidenced

  • Treat every participant with respect; harassment, discrimination, threats, and sexual misconduct are prohibited.
  • Offer only vetted creative instruction within the approved trial category and your actual ability.
  • Meet only at an approved public campus location or through an agreed approved-host HTTPS video link.
  • Do not share another participant's identity, contact details, session notes, or media without explicit permission.
  • Do not bypass credit, verification, blocking, moderation, or safety controls.
  • Use the in-product report and dispute tools when available; urgent danger should be directed to emergency or campus safety services.
