Safety & Liability Waiver

Version 2026-07-13-draft · External legal review: not yet evidenced

1. Physical Activities

This trial is limited to vetted creative instruction. Medical, clinical, weapons, illegal, and higher-risk physical instruction are prohibited. Sessions may occur through an approved video host or at an approved public campus location.

2. Assumption of Risk

Participants should stop or leave any session that feels unsafe. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or campus safety; Relay is not an emergency-response service.

3. Release of Liability

You release, waive, and covenant not to sue Relay, its founders, and affiliates from any liability arising from any Relay-arranged session.

4. Indemnification

You agree to indemnify Relay from claims arising from your participation or violation of terms.

5. Emergency limitations

Relay does not monitor sessions in real time and cannot authorize care for participants. Contact emergency services directly when immediate help is needed.

6. Safety Guidelines

  • Meet in public campus spaces only
  • Tell someone where you are going
  • Warm up and assess the space
  • Know your limits — stop if unsafe
  • No medical or clinical skills
  • No alcohol or drugs
  • Report unsafe behavior through the in-product report tool; use the published support contact if you cannot sign in

7. No Medical Advice

Nothing on Relay constitutes medical advice. Consult a healthcare provider before any physical program.

8. Independent Users

All users are independent participants, not employees or agents of Relay.

Draft notice. This is a working safety policy, not legal advice. External legal review and a named response owner remain required before launch.
