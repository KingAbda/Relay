Real answers

Stuff people actually ask.

01Does it cost anything?

Nope. Joining is free and no money moves between students. You pay with your time, period.

02How do the credits work?

Simple. Teach one thirty-minute session, you get one credit. Spend that credit to book a thirty-minute session with anyone else on the platform.

03What can I actually teach?

For this first round, creative stuff only — design, music, writing, photography, things like that. Nothing medical, nothing dangerous, nothing sketchy.

04Who gets in?

About twenty NYU students, picked by hand, each with a real school email. We're not opening the floodgates yet.

05Does the interest list mean I'm in?

No. Everyone on the list gets the form when it opens. Then we pick a group that makes sense — matching up what people can teach with what people want to learn.
