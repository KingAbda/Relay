About Relay

Trading skills, not money.

A peer-to-peer skill exchange built by students, for students — so learning something new never depends on what you can afford.

The idea

A structured way to trade what you know

No cash, just time. Thirty minutes of teaching earns one credit; spend one credit on another 30-minute session. Full trial access requires an invitation and verified NYU email.

The trial

Evidence before expansion

Relay is preparing a small, invite-only NYU trial in one creative category. The product will remain closed until its engineering, safety, privacy, operator, and recovery gates have current evidence.

The mission

Turn campus favor culture into fair culture

Every student has something to teach and something to learn. Relay connects them directly — without the awkwardness of putting a price on your knowledge.

The vision

Start at NYU, expand campus by campus

A world where any student can access any skill they want to learn — paid forward through teaching, not paid for in cash.

Abdurrahman Touray — Founder

First-generation Gambian American, NYU 2026 (HEOP Scholar). Relay began with friends informally exchanging skills without money and a question: what if there were a fair, structured system for this?

The next step is a controlled creative-skills trial designed to test real usefulness without outrunning participant safety, privacy, or operational readiness.
