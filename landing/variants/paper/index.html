Honest answers

The questions students ask first.

01Is Relay free?

Joining is free. Students trade credits for sessions, so no cash changes hands between peers.

02How do credits work?

Teach one 30-minute session to earn one credit. Spend one credit to book a 30-minute session with another student.

03What can I teach?

The controlled trial accepts vetted creative-skill listings only. High-risk, medical, weapons-related, illegal, or dangerously physical instruction is prohibited.

04Who can join?

The first cohort is limited to approximately 20 manually selected NYU participants who verify their institutional email.

05Does the interest list guarantee a place?

No. Interested people will receive a form, and the trial team will select a balanced cohort based on eligibility, availability, and compatible skill interests.
