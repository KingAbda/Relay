Honest answers

The questions students ask first.

01Is Relay free?

Joining is free, and no cash moves between students. You pay in time.

02How do credits work?

Teach one thirty-minute session to earn one credit. Spend that credit to book a thirty-minute session with another student.

03What can I teach?

Creative skills only for the first trial — design, music, writing, photography and the like. Anything medical, weapons-related, illegal or physically risky is out.

04Who can join?

The first cohort is about twenty NYU students, chosen by hand, each with a verified school email address.

05Does the interest list guarantee a place?

No. We will select a cohort that balances what people can teach, what they want to learn and when they are available.
