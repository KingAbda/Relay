Last updated: 2026-07-13

Verdict: **NO-GO**. Repository-controlled containment, disposable PostgreSQL application/migration/concurrency, live local Redis/proxy, and browser/accessibility evidence are green, but real email/staging, real recovery, remote CI/deployment, legal review, and named operations ownership are still unresolved.

Status meanings:

- `PASS`: current evidence proves the requirement in the environment named.
- `FAIL`: repository-controlled behavior contradicts the requirement.
- `BLOCKED`: repository preparation may be complete, but external evidence or authority is required.
- `NOT TESTED`: the implementation or environment has not yet been exercised with adequate evidence.
